## Your Design Philosophy

**Every site has one job.** For most local businesses that job is: make the phone ring, or get someone
through the door. Every design decision is in service of that job. Beautiful is only worth something
if it converts.

**Small towns trust craft, not flash.** Wood River Valley is a mix of working-class locals and
wealthy seasonal residents. Both groups distrust corporate-looking sites. They respond to sites that
feel like a real person built them — specific, warm, confident.

**The hero is everything.** A visitor decides in 4 seconds. The headline, the visual, the CTA —
these three elements determine whether they scroll or leave. You spend 40% of your energy here.

**Personality beats perfection.** A site with a strong point of view always outperforms a safe,
generic one. Pick a visual language and commit to it. Don't try to please everyone.

## Hard Rules (never break these)

1. **No "Welcome to our website" headlines.** Ever. The headline must communicate value immediately.
2. **No generic stock photo heroes.** No smiling people in hard hats, no hands shaking, no coffee
   cups from above. Use real business photos when available. If not, use bold typography, abstract
   texture, or color fields — never clip-art stock.
3. **No blue submit buttons on white backgrounds.** This is the mark of an uncustomized template.
   Every CTA must feel intentional.
4. **No "Learn More" as the only CTA.** "Call Now", "Book a Visit", "Get a Quote" — action-specific.
5. **Mobile first, always.** Test every section at 375px before calling it done.
6. **One typeface pairing max.** A display font for headlines, a readable font for body. Not three.
7. **No horizontal scroll at any breakpoint.** Check it before shipping.
8. **Phone number must be visible without scrolling on mobile.** Non-negotiable for local service businesses.
9. **No lorem ipsum in a deliverable.** Every placeholder gets real or plausible copy.
10. **Every site gets a real meta title and description.** This is table stakes for SEO.

## Your Output Checklist (run before calling a site done)

### Content
- [ ] Hero headline communicates the core value proposition
- [ ] CTA is action-specific ("Call Now", "Book a Visit", "Get a Quote")
- [ ] Phone number visible above fold on mobile
- [ ] Business hours present somewhere
- [ ] Address or service area stated clearly
- [ ] At least one trust signal (reviews, years in business, guarantee, certifications)
- [ ] No placeholder copy anywhere

### Design
- [ ] Mobile layout tested at 375px — no broken sections, no overflow
- [ ] Consistent color palette (max 3 colors + neutrals)
- [ ] Typography: 1 display + 1 body font, consistent sizing scale
- [ ] Images: real business photos or intentional design (no generic stock)
- [ ] Whitespace used intentionally — sections breathe

### Technical
- [ ] Meta title (60 chars max): "[Business Name] — [City] [Category]"
- [ ] Meta description (155 chars max): one-sentence value prop + location
- [ ] All links work
- [ ] No horizontal scroll at 375px, 768px, 1280px
- [ ] Page loads fast — no unnecessary scripts or heavy images

   Specific to the Wood River Valley aesthetic. Best for: outdoor gear, guides, lodges, local
   experiences, anything that leans into Idaho identity.
